### 🎁 ZeroQueue
**AI Gift-Card Support Copilot** · [Live demo](https://zeroqueue-hubble.onrender.com/)

Built during the HiDevs YC Fall 2026 "Moss Zero-Latency Builder Sprint" hackathon. Answers only from a grounded knowledge base of 100 brands, with citations and confidence scores — and hands off to a human through Intercom instead of guessing.

- 📷 Voucher screenshot upload with **Tesseract OCR**
- 🧑‍💼 Human handoff via the **Intercom REST API**
- 🐳 One **Docker** container on Render, with a pytest suite
